## 📚 **Luo Dictionary Learning**

### **Essential Luo Words Being Learned**
The system automatically learns these essential Luo words from Glosbe:

#### **Greetings & Basic**
- `inadi` → hello
- `ber` → good
- `erokamano` → thank you
- `mos` → sorry/excuse me
- `nying` → name

#### **Family Terms**
- `mama` → mother
- `baba` → father
- `nyako` → girl/daughter
- `wuod` → boy/son
- `dala` → home/compound

#### **Daily Life**
- `chiemo` → food
- `pi` → water
- `wang` → light/sun
- `odiechieng` → morning
- `otieno` → evening

#### **Actions**
- `dhi` → go
- `bi` → come
- `nindo` → sleep
- `tich` → work
- `wuok` → exit/leave
